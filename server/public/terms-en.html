1. About MEDICARD

MEDICARD's features may include:

  • MEDIVET — a text-based AI assistant for pets (not a veterinary diagnosis);
  • managing your health history;
  • storing and organizing medical test results;
  • informational explanations of test results;
  • managing medications;
  • medication reminders;
  • menstrual cycle tracking;
  • estimated cycle and ovulation predictions;
  • logging symptoms and pain;
  • step and physical activity data;
  • hydration tracking;
  • weight history;
  • daily health records;
  • summaries of health information;
  • preparing or exporting information for a doctor;
  • sharing certain information with a person the user chooses;
  • showing information about medication prices and availability in pharmacies;
  • other health-related digital features.

MEDICARD's features may be changed, added or removed as the application develops.

Unless the description of a specific feature expressly states otherwise, the information provided by MEDICARD is not a medical diagnosis or an individual treatment prescription.

4. MEDICARD is not an emergency service

MEDICARD is not intended for emergency medical care.

If you think that you or another person may have a life-threatening or health-threatening condition, do not wait for a response from MEDICARD or Medi.

Contact the emergency service for your location or go to a medical facility immediately.

MEDICARD does not continuously monitor the user's health condition and cannot automatically detect an emergency.

5. Medi — the artificial intelligence assistant

MEDICARD may include an assistant based on artificial intelligence — Medi.

Medi can help the user with health-related information by:

  • finding it;
  • organizing it;
  • explaining it;
  • summarizing it;
  • helping to understand it better;
  • spotting certain trends in the user's data.

Medi's answers may be generated using artificial intelligence models.

6. Limitations of artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence is not infallible.

Medi may:

  • misunderstand a question;
  • take the context into account only partially;
  • make a factual error;
  • misinterpret data;
  • present outdated information;
  • produce an answer that does not fit a particular user's condition.

Accordingly, especially important health decisions should not be based solely on an answer generated by Medi.

Before making a medical decision, consult an appropriately qualified professional.

7. AI explanations of medical tests

MEDICARD may offer the user automated or AI-based explanations of medical test results.

The purpose of such an explanation is to present complex medical information in a more understandable form.

However:

An AI explanation is not a diagnosis.

The assessment of laboratory results often depends on many factors, including:

  • age;
  • sex;
  • health history;
  • current conditions;
  • medications;
  • pregnancy;
  • the method used by the laboratory;
  • the relevant reference range;
  • other clinical data.

Therefore, the final medical assessment of test results should be made by a qualified healthcare professional.

10. Menstrual cycle tracking

MEDICARD may help the user record and analyze their menstrual cycle.

The application may calculate:

  • the estimated date of the next period;
  • the estimated cycle length;
  • the estimated ovulation window;
  • the estimated fertile window;
  • other cycle-related indicators.

These predictions are based on the data entered by the user and on mathematical/software models.

11. Cycle predictions are not a method of contraception

MEDICARD's cycle, ovulation and fertility predictions should not be used as a guaranteed method of preventing pregnancy.

A person's menstrual cycle can change because of many factors.

MEDICARD cannot guarantee that a predicted ovulation or fertile date will exactly match the actual period.

For questions about planning or preventing pregnancy, consult an appropriate healthcare professional.
